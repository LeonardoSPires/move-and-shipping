Glossary

Common terms in international moving

Door to Door

Service with pickup at origin and delivery to the final address.

Port to Port

Port-to-port service without residential pickup or delivery included.

LCL

Shared cargo in an ocean container.

FCL

Exclusive container for a single shipment.

Bill of Lading

Ocean transport document used for shipment.

Customs Clearance

Customs clearance process in the destination country.

DTHC

Handling charges applied at the destination terminal.

Inventory List

List of transported goods, usually used for insurance and customs.

Moving to Brazil

How to organize a move to Brazil

Anyone returning to Brazil or moving to the country should plan in advance. Document preparation, the inventory list, transport method and destination clearance are essential to avoid delays.

The first step is to understand the client profile, origin, type of goods and estimated arrival. With this information, the process can be guided and documents prepared more safely.

International checklist

International moving checklist

  • Define the destination country and estimated moving date.
  • Gather personal documents, proof documents and required authorizations.
  • Create a list of the goods to be transported.
  • Declare fragile items, artwork, electronics and high-value goods.
  • Evaluate insurance, storage and delivery timeline.
Costs

What influences the cost of a move?

The cost of a move may vary depending on volume, weight, origin, destination, transport mode, contracted service, packing, insurance, storage, port charges, destination costs and special requirements.

That is why a survey or detailed item list is important to estimate volume and prepare a more accurate proposal.

Insurance

How moving insurance works

Insurance helps protect belongings during transportation. Usually, an item list and declared values are required for proper coverage.

Allowed items

What can and cannot be transported

In general, moves transport used personal and household goods. Perishable, flammable, illegal, dangerous or restricted products may be prohibited or require specific review.

Documentation

Documents for moving to Brazil

Documents vary depending on the client profile, time abroad, immigration status and type of goods shipped. Proper guidance helps prevent clearance delays.

Moving from the United States to Brazil

A move between the USA and Brazil usually involves pickup, packing, international transportation, documentation, clearance in Brazil and final delivery. Advance planning is essential.

Frequently asked questions

Common moving questions

How long does an international move take?

The timeline depends on origin, destination, transport mode and document clearance.

Can I ship used furniture to Brazil?

Yes, as long as the process follows applicable rules and required documentation.

Is moving insurance mandatory?

It is not mandatory in every case, but it is highly recommended.

How does packing work?

Professional packing uses appropriate materials to protect furniture, electronics and fragile items.

Can my move be stored?

Yes. Storage can be evaluated based on timeline, location and service type.

Do I need to create an item list?

Yes. The list helps with organization, insurance, customs and item checking.

Can a commercial move be done in phases?

Yes. Commercial moves can be planned in phases to reduce operational impact.

What is unaccompanied baggage?

It is the shipment of personal belongings that travel separately from the passenger, usually in an international move.
